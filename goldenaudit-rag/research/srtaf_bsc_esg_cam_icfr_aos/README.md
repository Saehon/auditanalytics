## Research question
How do contractual and sustainability-related risks become visible, transformed, contested, or absent as they travel between strategic performance measurement (BSC), ESG management and reporting, financial-reporting controls (ICFR), and auditor CAM communication?

## SRTAF model
Economic rights and sustainability commitments → managerial BSC and ESG interpretation → accounting classification and reporting → control verification → selective CAM/KAM communication (only when applicable) → possible feedback to organizational management control. The pathway is **conditional, nonlinear and falsifiable**. ICFR and sustainability controls have distinct scopes.

## Status / safeguards
- **Proposed pilot**: 50 authentic, deduplicated, high-tech customer contracts; **0 verified** in the current local study.
- **Available documentary feasibility basis**: three authentic SEC comment-response records concerning Lightspeed Commerce (IFRS 15), Cloudera (ASC 606), and National Instruments (ASC 606), with 10 provisional textual units.
- **Not performed**: independent human coding, actual BSC observations, expert-adjudicated CAM–ICFR linkages, confidential ERP testing, or matched AI-agent experiments.
- **Do not infer**: contract-level compliance, ICFR weakness from a CAM, BSC adoption from public KPIs, economic causation from text co-occurrence, or auditor authority from AI outputs.
