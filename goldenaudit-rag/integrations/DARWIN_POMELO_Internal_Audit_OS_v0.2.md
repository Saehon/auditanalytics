# GoldenAudit-RAG V3 × DARWIN–POMELO Internal Audit OS — Integration v0.2
**Research-only architecture addendum · 20 November 2026 · Principal Investigator: Saeid Homayoun, University of Gävle**

## Scope and system boundary
This is a design/reproducibility integration, not an independently validated audit assistant or a working hosted multi-agent system. The GoldenAudit-RAG benchmark remains an independent evaluation track. No confidential cases, private repository registry, sealed gold labels, licensed examination questions, company financial records or credential secrets are published here.

## Constitutional architecture
The system retains two permanent cores: (1) **Stable Knowledge Core** preserving audit standards, evidence concepts and scientific meaning, and (2) **Replaceable Technology Core** containing provider-specific AI tooling and connectors. Four operational cores are Knowledge, Science, Technology, and Adversarial/Synthetic Digital Twin. Model changes may not rewrite accounting standards or silently alter review authority.

## Dual judgment brain
**Assurance / left brain:** IFRS accounting checks; ICFR and ITGC internal-control testing; cyber risk (identity, ransomware, incident response, resilience, vendor exposure); broader enterprise-risk control universe including financial, operational, fraud, ESG, legal, strategic and AI/model governance.

**Value / right brain:** economic-value diagnosis and six IIRC capitals: financial, manufactured, intellectual, human, social and relationship, and natural. Capital metrics preserve their units, period and stakeholder; descriptive change is not a causal estimate of audit value or a monetized aggregate.

## Evidence-governed pipeline
1. Case data-rights, privacy and sealed-holdout screen.
2. Authority/version/jurisdiction/effective-date applicability check.
3. Deterministic-first accounting, cyber and control tests.
4. Rights-cleared source retrieval and documented provenance.
5. Specialist audit reasoning, independent verification, contradiction screening (future model workflows).
6. VERA-style Evidence Passport and DARWIN V9-style claim falsification.
7. Human decision gate for professional audit conclusions.
8. Blinded, independent GoldenAudit expert evaluation on sealed held-out cases.
9. Failure taxonomy, scientific reproducibility ledger and five reporting tables.

## Research arms and outcomes
- A0: matched closed-book LLM.
- A1: true hybrid BM25 + dense RAG.
- A2: A1 with independent evidence verifier.
- A3: coordinated specialists with independent challenge and professional authorization.
- A4: optional preregistered GraphRAG extension.

**As of v0.2, none of A0–A4 have been run by the local integrated prototype.** The local bridge implements deterministic IFRS/ICFR/cyber/six-capital triage plus rights-, hash-, jurisdiction- and date-gated **BM25 lexical retrieval only**. It is not a hybrid RAG system and does not call an LLM.

The primary preregistered outcome for future real experiments is *SupportedAuthorized*, scored only through substantive correctness, valid evidence, appropriate qualification and authorized action. Pair arms at case level, cluster by scenario family, and preregister contrasts; do not mix this benchmark with firm-year economic or cyber data.

## Gold QA safeguards
V3 reported 3,207 **provisional candidate** cases and an unscored 60-case pilot; they are not 3,207 human-approved Golden Answers. Reviewers must be qualified and blinded; gold keys and scoring rubrics must never enter retrieval/dev indexes; rights-cleared cases only. Software checks cannot authenticate human experts.

## First executable local prototype checkpoint
Local v0.2 package (not published as code in this GitHub path) passed **23 standard-library unit tests**, including nested gold-field exclusion, rights, effective-date, jurisdiction, source-hash and professional gate checks. An original synthetic manufacturing case generated 15 proposed exception signals; 3 original synthetic documents were eligible for BM25 retrieval; 2/2 synthetic behavior-smoke fixtures passed. No empirical LLM comparisons, professional audit conclusions, human adjudication or independent assurance have been conducted.

Five blank research table schemas are available locally: (1) sample and gold adjudication, (2) arm outcomes/correlations, (3) main regression, (4) robustness/ablation, (5) safety/error/cost frontier. **All scientific result tables are empty.**

## Cross-project contracts
Interfaces connect, subject to verified rights and source access, AuditGold, POMELO/VERA, DARWIN V9, KIWI CAM/KAM, ICFR/SOX, CyberRiskBERT, ESG, Forensic/CFE, JMIS agent governance, MACEI and Swedish SMEs.
