## Provenance and implementation status
This is a public **integration record**, not proof of an IIA-endorsed audit engine. It preserves the V0.2 system's two stable constitutional cores (knowledge and replaceable technology), four operational cores (Knowledge, Science, Technology, Adversarial/Digital Twin), the IFRS/ICFR/Cyber Assurance Brain, the IIRC six-capital Value Brain, and GoldenAudit-RAG's sealed research benchmark boundaries.

## The principal offline service
`StandardsService.assess_engagement_standards(engagement_id)` takes an explicitly registered engagement profile, the synthetic audit case and a versioned policy pack. It returns framework dispositions (APPLIES, NOT_YET_EFFECTIVE, MANUAL_APPLICABILITY_REVIEW, ADOPTED_REFERENCE, REFERENCE_ONLY, etc.), mapped **original illustrative** objectives, deterministic test signals, evidence metadata, explicit exclusions pending independent approval, cyber→ICFR risk hypotheses and an always-blocked professional-release gate.

The engine registers 12 framework entries and 17 newly written illustrative control objectives, **not the 17 COSO principles or an official licensed IIA requirements inventory**. It preserves version/date boundaries and does not embed copyrighted standards clauses.

## IIA applicability verified from official source pages (as of 20 November 2026)
- Global Internal Audit Standards: effective **9 January 2025** — https://www.theiia.org/en/content/standards/complete-global-internal-audit-standards/
- Cybersecurity Topical Requirement: effective **5 February 2026** — https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/topical-requirements/cybersecurity/
- Third-Party Topical Requirement: effective **15 September 2026** — https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/topical-requirements/third-party/
- Organizational Behavior: **future-effective 15 December 2026** — https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/topical-requirements/organizational-behavior/
- Organizational Resilience: **future-effective 30 April 2027** — https://www.theiia.org/en/standards/2024-standards/topical-requirements/organizational-resilience/

Topical Requirements apply only when effective and in scope for the assurance engagement, with risk-assessed applicability and documented exceptions. SOX Sections 302/404 require verified US regulatory scope. PCAOB AS 2201 is an external-audit reference, not automatically an internal-audit obligation. COSO, NIST CSF 2.0 and the Integrated Reporting Framework are assessed as selected reference criteria rather than universally binding regulations.
